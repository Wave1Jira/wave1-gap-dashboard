Summary & ranking — current walkthrough list

Only three communities actually offer a 1 BR + den or loft

That is the headline finding of this round. Of the ten on the list, Reveal Hackensack, The Walcott and EVO Englewood are the only ones with a genuine one-bedroom-plus-den or loft layout. Highlands at Westwood offers a den, but only in its two-bedroom, which lands at $3,440+. The other six run studio / 1 BR / 2 BR with no flex room at all — though The Monarch's largest one-bedroom (1,120 sf) and 140 Mayhill's (up to 1,300 sf) are den-sized without carrying the name or the premium.

On price, standard one-bedrooms cluster tightly at $2,300–$2,700 across the whole list. A true 1 BR + den runs roughly $3,000–$3,100 — about a $500/month premium that puts you within a few hundred dollars of a two-bedroom at several of these. Worth asking each leasing agent directly whether the 2 BR pencils out better than the den.

Reveal takes the top spot: it confirms den and loft plans on select layouts, has one of the better ratings on the list (4.3), a resort pool with sun deck, a dog run with direct access to Hackensack River County Park, no pet weight limit, the shortest Yonkers commute, and a tour that is already booked for Thursday at 3:15. The Walcott follows: brand new (2023), loft homes and townhomes in the layout mix, the most accommodating pet policy here (two pets up to 75 lbs each), and up to two months free with an August 15 move-in deadline attached. EVO is third — the only community with a dedicated, priced 1 BR + Den plan on the board, but at $3,083 it sits above the cap.

Two cautions worth carrying into the tours. 140 Mayhill restricts residents to one pet with a 20 lb weight limit, which likely rules it out on both counts before you walk the door. And The Parkview at Polifly is the outlier on quality — a 1967 building with a 2.6 rating across only twelve reviews, no published office hours, and no confirmed Saturday availability. Call before spending a Saturday slot on it.

Where they sit relative to each other

All ten sit in Bergen County, spanning about 13 miles from East Rutherford in the south to Westwood in the north. Four cluster in and around downtown Hackensack — The Forte, The Walcott, Parkview at Polifly and Reveal — with The Forte and The Walcott two minutes apart. Volair Wesmont anchors the southwest in Wood-Ridge; The Monarch is the southernmost point in East Rutherford; Saddle Brook and Rochelle Park sit west on the Route 17 corridor; EVO is the eastern outlier in Englewood; Highlands at Westwood is the northern end and the closest to 287.
