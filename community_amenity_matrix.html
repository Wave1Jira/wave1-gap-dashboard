Entry prices are mostly base rent and exclude mandatory monthly fees, parking and usage-billed utilities, which vary widely by community and can add roughly $75–250 a month. The one community in this set that publishes a full fee stack discloses a monthly lifestyle fee, trash hauling and a utility billing admin charge on top of base rent, so assume something similar everywhere and ask each office for a total monthly figure, itemized.

Reading the matrix

A few patterns worth knowing before booking anything.

✓ Where a flex room genuinely exists

Pavion publishes Live Work Lofts with combined live/work space up to 1,200 sf. Parkside lists a home office in most units as a standard feature. Lexica offers optional dens on select plans. Normandy's two-bedroom carries a den plus a garage. Avalon has lofts per resident accounts, though they aren't marketed as a tier — name the plan when booking.

✗ Worth confirming before you drive

Highlands is credited with loft layouts in some sources, but the management company's own materials list one, two and three bedroom only — one call settles it. Jeanne Marie has in-unit laundry in select homes only. Parkside mentions no EV charging anywhere. Highlands charges parking per vehicle rather than including it.

Geography, roughly: three of these sit within about two miles of each other in Nanuet (Highlands, Jeanne Marie, Normandy). Pavion and Parkside are both on the Hudson, about ten miles apart, with Parkside furthest north in Haverstraw. Lexica and Avalon Montville are the New Jersey pair, about six miles apart and roughly 20–25 miles southwest of the Rockland group. The Nanuet three can be done in one trip; the river pair pairs naturally; New Jersey is its own outing.
